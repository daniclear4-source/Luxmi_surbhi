Why Should You Trust Us?

Trust Through Transparency (8 Pillars)

We don't just ask for trust; we build it through our actions, systems, and ethical boundaries:

1. Clear Identity

Every authorised volunteer/member working with seniors should be identifiable.

2. Responsible Volunteer

Volunteers will be assigned responsibilities according to their role and capability.

3. Privacy

Personal, medical and family information shared by seniors will be treated confidentially.

4. No False Promises

We will clearly communicate what we can and cannot provide.

5. Accountability

Activities and support provided will be properly documented.

6. Family Communication

Where appropriate and with consent, important concerns can be communicated to the designated family/emergency contact.

7. Financial Transparency

Any charges, donations or programme contributions will be clearly communicated and documented.

8. Complaint & Feedback

Seniors and their families will have a direct channel to provide feedback or raise concerns.
