Community-grounded initiatives designed to serve with dignity, support with compassion, and stand by our seniors.

Home Projects & Campaigns
Active Programs

Ongoing Initiatives

Core community programs actively functioning in Chakradharpur and Jamshedpur:

Ongoing Project Jharkhand

Senior Companionship & Friendly Visits

Volunteer-driven home visits and companionship sessions designed to ensure that no senior citizen is left feeling isolated or lonely. Focuses on conversation, spending meaningful time, and listening.

Focus: Emotional Support & Loneliness Reduction
Ongoing Project Jharkhand

Regular Well-Being Check-In Network

Structured welfare check-ins via scheduled phone calls and authorised volunteer visits, with an escalation mechanism to connect with designated family and emergency contacts when necessary.

Focus: Well-Being Monitoring & Emergency Linkage
Ongoing Project Jharkhand

Digital Support & Smartphone Assistance

Patient, step-by-step guidance for seniors on using smartphones, WhatsApp, digital payment awareness, and booking online medical appointments.

Focus: Digital Literacy & Independence
Targeted Awareness

Community Campaigns & Drives

Focused campaigns creating awareness and building stronger circles of safety:

Active Campaign Safety

Senior Safety & Scam Prevention Drive

Workshops and one-on-one sessions educating seniors and their families on phone fraud, financial scams, basic home safety, and emergency protocols.

Active Campaign Health Linkage

Doctor Appointment & Healthcare Linkage

Facilitating seamless connection between seniors and local healthcare professionals, assisting with appointment schedules, and promoting health-awareness activities.

Active Campaign Recreation

Festival Celebrations & Get-Togethers

Organizing cultural programmes, festive gatherings, recreational games, and outings where senior citizens interact, share stories, and celebrate community spirit.

Future Roadmap

Community Expansion Framework

“Our ultimate goal is to build a community-based support system for senior citizens, beginning at the local level and gradually expanding wherever possible.”

We welcome collaborations with volunteers, medical facilitators, local institutions, and partner organizations to broaden our circle of care.
