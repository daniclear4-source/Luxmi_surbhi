Direct Social Impact

Where Your Support Goes

Every contribution directly aids our community-based senior support system:

Companionship & Home Visits

Enables regular friendly visits, listening sessions, and emotional care for isolated elders across Chakradharpur and Jamshedpur.

Welfare Check-In System

Supports routine well-being phone calls, welfare check-in protocols, and emergency coordination with designated family contacts.

Cultural & Festival Celebrations

Funds community get-togethers, recreational games, outings, and festive occasions that reconnect seniors with their neighborhoods.

Trust Through Transparency

Our Financial Transparency Commitment

Official Policy: “Any charges, donations or programme contributions will be clearly communicated and documented.”

In accordance with our ethical code, Laxmi Surbhi NGO strictly prohibits unauthorized borrowing, lending, solicitation, or untracked financial dealings. All donor contributions are acknowledged and documented transparently.
