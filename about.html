Who We Are

A Second Family for Every Senior

Laxmi Surbhi NGO is a registered non-governmental organization (Registration Number: 690), established in 2007. Our Senior Care & Support Initiative is dedicated to ensuring that senior citizens are not left feeling neglected, isolated, helpless or alone.

We operate across Chakradharpur and Jamshedpur, Jharkhand. We believe that growing older should never mean losing one's dignity, independence, companionship or sense of belonging.

Our approach is based on a simple thought: “Every senior deserves someone who listens, someone who cares, and someone who stands beside them.”

Our Guiding Light

Our Vision

“A society where every senior citizen lives with dignity, security, companionship and a sense of belonging.”

WE ENVISION A FUTURE WHERE:

No senior feels forgotten or socially isolated.
Every senior is treated with dignity and respect.
Seniors have access to appropriate support when they need it.
Physical, emotional and social well-being receives attention.
Seniors remain connected with their families and communities.
Their experience, knowledge and wisdom are valued.
Age is never considered a reason to be excluded from society.
Our Daily Commitment

Our Mission

“To serve, support and empower senior citizens by providing compassionate companionship, meaningful engagement, assistance in accessing essential services, health and wellness support, and a dependable community network.”

SUPPORT

Provide practical and emotional support according to individual needs.

CONNECT

Reduce loneliness by creating meaningful human connections.

ASSIST

Help seniors and their families navigate appropriate healthcare and essential services.

PROMOTE WELL-BEING

Encourage healthy, active and socially connected ageing.

Ethical Principles

Our Seniors Deserve Dignity

Every person associated with the Laxmi Surbhi programme must strictly follow these 8 fundamental principles:

RESPECT

Never humiliate, insult or discriminate.

PRIVACY

Do not share personal information or photographs without appropriate permission.

SAFETY

Never place a senior in an unsafe situation.

HONESTY

Never misuse the trust placed in us.

BOUNDARIES

Volunteers must maintain appropriate professional and personal boundaries.

NO EXPLOITATION

No unauthorised borrowing, lending, solicitation or financial dealings with beneficiaries.

CONSENT

Respect the senior's choices and independence.

ACCOUNTABILITY

Report concerns through the proper organisational channel.

“Our commitment to ethics and dignity is the foundation of every relationship we build. Because respect is not optional — it is our promise.”

About The Members

  • Respect fellow members and beneficiaries.
  • Work collaboratively.
  • Take responsibility for assigned duties.
  • Maintain transparency and integrity.
