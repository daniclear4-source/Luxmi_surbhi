Official Code

Volunteer Code of Conduct

Every authorised volunteer must commit to our 8 ethical principles:

  • Respect: Never humiliate, insult or discriminate.
  • Privacy: Keep personal and medical data strictly confidential.
  • Safety: Never place a senior in an unsafe situation.
  • Honesty: Never misuse the trust placed in us.
  • Boundaries: Maintain appropriate professional and personal boundaries.
  • No Exploitation: Zero unauthorized borrowing or financial dealings.
  • Consent: Respect senior choices and independence.
  • Accountability: Report concerns through proper organizational channels.
